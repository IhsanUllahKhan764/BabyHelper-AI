Introduction

Infant care introduces significant maternal anxiety and stress, especially in urban Pakistani environments transitioning rapidly from joint family systems to isolated nuclear units. Parents regularly struggle to accurately interpret non-verbal distress signals, avoid manual tracking errors of growth milestones, and implement proper weaning metrics.

Our project resolves this operational vulnerability by introducing an intelligent mobile application. BabyHelper AI executes edge-based audio deep learning alongside statistical child development frameworks directly on low-to-mid-range hardware, facilitating highly context-aware home healthcare assistance anytime and anywhere.

Problem Statement

Many parents operate without necessary structural intergenerational parenting support, exposing infants to vulnerabilities due to:

Data Fatigue

Parents must context-switch across multiple disjointed apps for tracking.

Subjective Evaluation

Baby cries are subjectively evaluated, masking critical distress anomalies like acute pain.

Irrelevant Guidelines

Malnutrition and stunting remain widespread due to non-localized, culturally irrelevant Western diet guidelines.

Key Objectives

Provide offline, privacy-preserving, AI-powered infant cry classification directly on the smartphone.

Implement clinical growth anomaly screening using automated WHO LMS statistical z-score formulas.

Deliver age-adaptive nutritional guidance.

Synthesize comprehensive daily and weekly sleep quality trend charts to help optimize infant sleep environments.
